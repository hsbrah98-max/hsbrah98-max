# Harman Brah, MD

Physician and researcher working in clinical AI, computational psychiatry, and pharmacovigilance. Principal investigator of **Brah Lab** at ASDRP.

[harmanbrah.com](https://harmanbrah.com/)

### Clinical AI & Pharmacovigilance

* **[psych-atypicality-eval](https://github.com/hsbrah98-max/psych-atypicality-eval)**: Evaluation benchmark and red-teaming suite for the atypicality problem in LLM-powered psychiatry. Companion to Garcia, Chua & Brah, *Journal of Medical Ethics* (2025).
* **[psych-discharge-eval](https://github.com/hsbrah98-max/psych-discharge-eval)**: Evaluation harness and fact-checker for LLM-written psychiatric discharge summaries.
* **[clinical-pdf-fact-guard](https://github.com/hsbrah98-max/clinical-pdf-fact-guard)**: Quote-verified summarizer for clinical and school intake records, with citation checks against the source PDF.
* **[cobenfy-faers-pharmacovigilance](https://github.com/hsbrah98-max/cobenfy-faers-pharmacovigilance)**: READUS-PV disproportionality analysis of xanomeline-trospium with concomitant antimuscarinics in FDA FAERS.
* **[glp1-antipsychotic-faers](https://github.com/hsbrah98-max/glp1-antipsychotic-faers)**: FAERS study of GLP-1 receptor agonist and second-generation antipsychotic interactions.
* **[glp1-vegfr2-molecular-dynamics](https://github.com/hsbrah98-max/glp1-vegfr2-molecular-dynamics)**: 100 ns GROMACS simulations and MM-PBSA analysis of GLP-1 receptor agonists against VEGFR2.

### Research & Computational Tools (Brah Lab Output)

Code written by Brah Lab students under my direction. Each repository stays on the student's own account.

* **[FBDD_ChemBERTa](https://github.com/Aayush-Kothari/FBDD_ChemBERTa)** by [Aayush Kothari](https://github.com/Aayush-Kothari): Transformer-based fragment prediction for fragment-based drug discovery. Published in *Journal of Cheminformatics* (2026), [doi:10.1186/s13321-026-01255-w](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13321-026-01255-w). *(Co-PI, research mentor & co-author)*
* **[DeepBERTa](https://github.com/Aayush-Kothari/DeepBERTa)** by [Aayush Kothari](https://github.com/Aayush-Kothari) and team: DeepSMILES-based chemical language model trained at billion-molecule scale and benchmarked on MoleculeNet. *(Co-PI & research mentor)*
* **[DeepSMILESCorrector](https://github.com/Aayush-Kothari/DeepSMILESCorrector)** by [Aayush Kothari](https://github.com/Aayush-Kothari): Transformer that repairs invalid SMILES and DeepSMILES strings. *(Co-PI & research mentor)*
* **[frag-predict-linker-t5](https://github.com/hsbrah98-max/frag-predict-linker-t5)** with Vihaan Udayakumar: T5 linker reconstruction compared across six molecular representations. *(Research mentor & co-author)*
* **[Prolonged ICU Stay & Mortality (MIMIC-III)](https://github.com/27nishap-collab/Prolonged-ICU-Stay-and-Mortality-Study-using-MIMIC-III)** by [Nisha Padhi](https://github.com/27nishap-collab): Do clinical notes improve early prediction of mortality or prolonged ICU stay? A replication and extension of Weissman et al. (2018). Submitted to medRxiv (October 2026). *(Research mentor & co-author)*
* **[GLP-1 RAs in Wet AMD](https://github.com/natashaprabhu/GLPs_WetAMD)** by [Natasha Prabhu](https://github.com/natashaprabhu): Computational analysis of GLP-1 receptor agonist binding to VEGFR2 as a candidate mechanism in neovascular AMD. *(Research mentor; manuscript in preparation)*
